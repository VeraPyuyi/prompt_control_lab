# 0.3.0a2 acceptance record

This preview adds research evidence import and execution diagnostics. Acceptance is
software verification, not a new scientific confirmation. New real research materials
remain in a private acceptance directory and are excluded from the repository,
distribution and pull request. Public demonstrations are synthetic.

| Check | Status | Evidence and scope |
| --- | --- | --- |

Original 20,000 draws, seeds, shared weights and 18/24/22 correction families are retained. Expected results are read after calculation. |
| Relocated private export | Passed | The product exported a completed bundle, imported it in a new directory and reran all five suites; all 52 interval checks passed again. Source files were not rewritten. |
| Research task recovery | Passed focused regression | Complete suites are reused; incomplete suites restart from frozen inputs. Cancellation, parent exit, lost queue ownership, incomplete reports and concurrent export/resume have dedicated checks. |

Saved-score replay does not reconstruct unavailable activations or original model calls.
An anonymous derivative's hash establishes content integrity, not the chronology of a
historical prediction. Unknown costs stay unknown. Small-panel operation timings are not
extrapolated into measured large-scale policy benefits. Local properties do not imply
model-wide guarantees.

The older 0.3.0a1 acceptance document remains a dated snapshot. New imports derive
completion and uncertainty from their supplied records; its earlier pending labels
are not applied to newly completed research bundles.
